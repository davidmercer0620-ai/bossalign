# SkyPulse Robotics: Dual-Stakeholder Principles & Negative Boundaries (Mock Case)

> Synthetic showcase demonstrating how BossAlign decouples and orthogonalizes preferences between multiple senior stakeholders.

## Stakeholder Persona Matrix

| Stakeholder | Role | Primary Focus & Aesthetic Lens | Critical Redlines |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Boss_Victor** | Founder / CEO | **Strategic intent, cinematic tone, pacing rhythm**<br>Rejects stale corporate video clichés; demands restraint, visceral impact, and understated confidence. | Buzzwords, feature laundry lists, announcer-style voiceovers, pompous adjectives. |

## Dual-Review Rules with Negative Boundaries

### Rule 01: Replace Vague Slogans with Grounded Testing (Boss_Victor)
- **Supporting Evidence**: `EVT-SKP-001`
- **Core Directive**: Eliminate abstract terms like *"industry pioneer"* or *"exquisite craftsmanship"*; prove capability with Level 7 wind-tunnel stress tests and heavy downpour footage.
- **Applicable Scope**: Flagship product launch films, hardcore engineering benchmark showcases.
- **Negative Boundary (Prohibited Contexts)**:
  > ❌ **Counter-Example**: Do NOT interpret this as deleting the company identity. Official certification slates at the intro/outro must retain standard branding.

### Rule 02: Keep Operational Workflows Grounded and Continuous (Lead_Alex)
- **Supporting Evidence**: `EVT-SKP-002`
- **Core Directive**: From opening the flight case to RTK base station pairing and compass calibration, shots must reflect genuine field pilot routines.
- **Applicable Scope**: Delivery onboarding walkthroughs, quick-start guides, QA handoff reels.
- **Negative Boundary (Prohibited Contexts)**:
  > ❌ **Counter-Example**: Do NOT turn a 30s dynamic hero video into a 10-minute tutorial. In high-tempo films, workflows serve as authentic background actions, not step-by-step pauses.
